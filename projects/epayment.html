What This Project Was About

TikTok Shop has grown into one of Vietnam's most active e-commerce platforms — and its payment infrastructure is what makes or breaks the buying experience. This project took a close look at how that infrastructure actually works: how money moves from a user tapping "Buy Now" all the way through to the seller getting paid.

The scope

We mapped the full payment ecosystem — customer layer, platform middleware, payment gateways (VNPay, Momo, ZaloPay), bank and e-wallet integrations, seller payout systems, and logistics-linked COD flows. Then we evaluated what works, what doesn't, and where AI can fix the gaps.

The final output included a SWOT analysis, five concrete AI improvement proposals, and a prioritized implementation roadmap ranked by technical complexity and user impact.

How the System Works

Before evaluating anything, we had to understand the architecture completely. The payment flow on TikTok Shop isn't a single step — it's a chain of systems that need to hand off correctly at every point.

  • Customer touchpoint — in-app checkout initiated from product page or livestream
  • Platform layer — TikTok Shop's own order management and payment orchestration
  • Payment gateway — routing to VNPay, Momo, ZaloPay, or card networks depending on user selection
  • Authorization & fraud detection — OTP verification, tokenization, real-time fraud scoring
  • Order confirmation & logistics — COD flagging, handoff to logistics partner for delivery-linked payment
  • Refund & settlement — reversal flows back through the same chain, with timelines varying by payment method
The COD problem

Cash on Delivery still dominates a large share of TikTok Shop transactions in Vietnam. That's not just a user behavior issue — it's a structural limitation. COD breaks the digital payment loop and makes fraud detection, refunds, and seller settlement significantly harder to automate.

Assessment: Strengths & Gaps

The SWOT analysis framed TikTok Shop's payment position honestly — the platform has real advantages, but also structural vulnerabilities that compound as the platform scales.

What's working:

  • Seamless in-app checkout with no redirect to external pages
  • Broad payment method coverage — COD, bank transfer, Momo, ZaloPay, Apple Pay, Google Pay
  • Multi-layer security: OTP, tokenization, device fingerprinting, and real-time fraud scoring
  • Livestream commerce tightly integrated with checkout — lower steps to purchase

Where the gaps are:

  • High COD dependency limits digital payment penetration and raises operational cost
  • Livestream speed creates fraud surface area — impulse purchases bypass careful verification
  • Refund timelines inconsistent across payment partners — damages trust on returns
  • No biometric authentication option — OTP alone is weaker against SIM-swap attacks
Five AI improvement proposals

AI fraud detection system · AI risk scoring engine · Biometric face verification replacing OTP · One-click checkout flow · Split payment functionality — each mapped to the specific gap it addresses, with expected benefit analysis.

What the Team Delivered

  • Full payment architecture map — end-to-end system diagram tracing every layer from customer to settlement
  • Payment flow documentation — step-by-step breakdown of the complete transaction lifecycle including fraud detection and refund paths
  • SWOT analysis — structured evaluation of TikTok Shop's payment position in Vietnam's cashless trajectory
  • 5 AI improvement proposals — each with a process flow diagram, UI mockup, and expected benefits analysis
  • Implementation roadmap — solutions prioritized by technical complexity and user impact, with rollout phasing
  • Final presentation deck — designed for clarity and visual cohesion across all sections

My Contribution

Specifically, I wrote:

  • The assessment of user experience strengths — checkout flow, payment method diversity, trust signals
  • The breakdown of security advantages: OTP flow, tokenization, real-time fraud monitoring
  • The disadvantage analysis: COD dependency, livestream fraud risk, refund inconsistency
  • The SWOT, framed in the context of Vietnam's cashless payment policy direction
  • Presentation design — structure, visual hierarchy, and layout for the full slide deck

What I Took Away

"Writing the assessment section taught me that a good critique isn't just listing problems — it's understanding why the problem exists and what it costs."

The most valuable part of this project was learning to read a payment system from the inside out. Most users interact with checkout as a black box — you tap, it works (or doesn't). Mapping the full architecture made clear how many moving parts have to succeed in sequence, and how a weakness in one layer — like COD dependency — can ripple through settlement, fraud detection, and refund timelines all at once.

Designing the presentation also pushed me to translate dense technical findings into something a non-technical audience could follow without losing precision. That balance is harder than it looks.
