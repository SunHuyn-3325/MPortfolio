My Story

Turning curiosity into impact

I'm Xuân Quyên, a 3rd-year E-Commerce student at the University of Economics and Law (UEL) in Ho Chi Minh City. My academic journey has been anything but ordinary — I've explored product management, user experience research, full-stack development, and business strategy, often within the same semester.

What drives me? I love understanding why people do what they do, and then designing systems and products that make their lives easier. I've led teams, written business requirements documents, shipped working software, and designed 27+ Figma screens — all while maintaining a 7.9 GPA.

Outside of academics, I'm a program leader, a volunteer, and a constant learner who believes the best way to grow is to do things that scare you a little.
